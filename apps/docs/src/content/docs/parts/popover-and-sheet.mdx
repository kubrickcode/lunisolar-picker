---
title: Popover and Sheet
description: Containers that open a picker next to its trigger or from the bottom of the screen.
---

```tsx
import { Calendar, Popover } from '@lunisolar-picker/react';

<Popover.Root>
  <Popover.Trigger render={<FilterChip />}>Due date</Popover.Trigger>
  <Popover.Content aria-label="Choose due date">
    <Calendar.Root value={value} onValueChange={(next) => setValue(next)} />
    <Popover.Close>Done</Popover.Close>
  </Popover.Content>
</Popover.Root>;
```

`Popover.Trigger` and `Popover.Close` take a `render` prop to use your own button. `Popover.Close` runs your `onClick` first, then closes unless you call `event.preventDefault()`. `Popover.Root` takes `open`, `defaultOpen`, `onOpenChange` and `placement` (default `'bottom-start'`).

| | Popover | Sheet |
|---|---|---|
| Role | `dialog` | `dialog`, `aria-modal="true"` |
| Name | Give `Content` an `aria-label` | Same |
| Opening | `Popover.Trigger` | Your own button sets `open` (there is no `Sheet.Trigger`) |
| Position | Next to the trigger, flips and shifts to stay on screen | Bottom of the viewport |
| Focus | Moves to the selected day or today, stays inside, returns to the trigger on close | Same |
| Closing | Escape, outside click, `Close` | Escape, backdrop tap, `Close` |
| Background | Interactive | `inert`, scroll locked |

Content is rendered in a portal at the end of `body` and unmounted when closed, so a calendar inside it starts fresh each time it opens. Put `.dark` or `data-theme` and your font on `html` or `body` so portal content inherits them.

The finished pickers pick a sheet automatically on narrow touch screens (`presentation="auto"`, which matches `(pointer: coarse) and (max-width: 640px)`). In your own compositions, `usePresentation('auto')` gives the same answer.

Swipe-to-close is not supported, because it competes with wheel scrolling inside the sheet.
